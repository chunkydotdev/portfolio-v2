# I Spent 100+ Hours Testing App Store Screenshots. Here's What Actually Gets Downloads.

**Most indie developers are DESTROYING their conversion rates with ugly screenshots. Don't be one of them.**

I've launched multiple apps and obsessed over every pixel of my App Store presence. After countless A/B tests and iterations, I cracked the code on what makes people actually tap that download button.

Here's my exact 5-step framework. Steal it.

---

## Step 1: Stop Using Basic Screenshots

You know what kills downloads? Flat, boring screenshots straight from your phone.

The first thing I do is gather my best screens from the XCode Simulator. Not random ones—the screens that show your app's **core value proposition** in under 2 seconds.

If someone can't understand what your app does from the first screenshot, you've already lost them.

---

## Step 2: The Mockup Secret Nobody Talks About

Here's where 90% of developers mess up.

They use flat device frames. Boring. Forgettable. Dead.

I use [mockuuups.studio](https://mockuuups.studio/) to grab dynamic iPhone mockups—slanted angles, dramatic perspectives, the works.

The key? **One angled hero shot + one clean front-facing shot.** This combo creates visual hierarchy that guides the eye exactly where you want it.

---

## Step 3: Photoshop for the Win (Yes, Really)

"But I'm a developer, not a designer!"

Doesn't matter. Learn the skew tool in Photoshop. It takes 10 minutes.

Drop your screenshot into that slanted mockup, hit Command+T, right-click > Skew, and match the perspective. That's it. That's the secret sauce.

For cleaner shots? Figma handles those perfectly.

---

## Step 4: The Gradient Background Hack

This is where amateur screenshots become professional ones.

**The formula:**
- Create a frame at 1242 x (2688 × number of screens)
- Add a diagonal gradient (top-left to bottom-right)
- Layer 2-3 radial gradients on top for depth

Why does this work? Because a connected gradient across all your screenshots makes your app page feel **premium and intentional.**

Your first screenshot should feature that angled hero mockup bleeding into the second frame. This creates curiosity. People WILL swipe.

Add icons (I use Lucide from Iconify—MIT licensed, free forever) and punchy one-liner captions on each screen.

---

## Step 5: The Photoshop Slice Trick

Export your entire Figma frame as one massive PNG.

Import into Photoshop. Use the Slice tool to cut it into individual screenshots.

Why? Because when you need to make changes (and you will), you re-export once and re-slice. Done. No manual cropping. No pixel math. No headaches.

---

## The Bottom Line

Your App Store screenshots are your storefront window. They're working 24/7, converting (or losing) potential users while you sleep.

**Stop treating them like an afterthought.**

Good screenshots won't save a bad app. But bad screenshots will absolutely kill a good one.
